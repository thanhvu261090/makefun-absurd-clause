Make.fun bounty entry: a single absurd clause buried deep inside a
terms-of-service agreement, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

> The user agrees that, by scrolling past this clause, they have legally
> adopted the company's office plant, including all future watering
> obligations.

149 characters, one sentence, within the 1–200 character gate.

The joke is the quiet escalation: it opens as ordinary consent language —
scrolling past a clause, the thing nobody reads — and ends with the reader
legally owning a houseplant and its watering schedule. The absurdity never
announces itself; it just sits there in the boilerplate, binding.
